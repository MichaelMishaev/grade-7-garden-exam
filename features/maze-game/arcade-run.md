# Short arcade run with bridge challenges and a boss

Delivery: implemented as an optional maze arcade page with an isolated per-tab save. Automated unit, actual browser expedition and independent QA evidence is recorded below. Production push is explicitly authorized by Michael after green applicable checks; exact deployment evidence is recorded by release SHA separately. Physical-device, manual screen-reader and student-enjoyment testing remain unverified.

## Problem and intended outcome

The maze needs immediate interactive goals after entry, beyond walking between questions. Give existing PrepX players a compact action arc: see the boss, earn three crystals, use them to break its shield, then receive a visible reward. The engagement hypothesis is that short physical challenges and a visible final opponent make a run more enjoyable; human playtesting is required to establish that, rather than treating completion tests as proof of retention.

The arcade entry is labelled in Hebrew as a three-question round; completing it never marks the full source quiz or the normal maze complete. Source questions/choices/correct answers/explanations/references are reused unchanged.

## Run structure and controls

Target roughly three minutes for a familiar player; this is a tuning target, not a deadline. Questions, reading, pause and retry have no forced completion timer. Show the boss behind a gate and the goal “אספו שלושה גבישים כדי להגיע לשומר”.

1. Select a subject and enter an arcade run. Pin three unique normalized, single-correct, game-compatible questions and choice orders from that bank for this run; do not attempt to auto-grade unsupported open/text tasks. A bank with fewer than three eligible questions cannot start the mode; offer the existing normal maze instead.
2. Complete bridge challenge 1, then answer question 1. The first answer locks. One transaction records the first answer, knowledge score/coins, completed stage, earned crystal and pending stage gift. Correct earns the existing 10 fictional coins; wrong earns none and costs one heart. Show the source explanation and award the crystal regardless of correctness. If the wrong answer removes the last heart, restore three hearts at the next safe checkpoint after feedback; do not repeat the answered bridge/question or omit its rewards.
3. Repeat for challenge/question 2 and 3. Reuse the same bridge mechanic with three authored timing/path variations, rather than requiring three new minigames. Each challenge has a safe starting tile and a reachable landing tile.
4. After all three questions and crystals, open the boss gate and enter a safe, untimed boss-ready area. Players can claim gifts, buy boosts, inspect the boss cue and deliberately press “מוכנים לבוס” before attacks begin. In three cycles, telegraph an attack, allow the player to dodge, then expose a shield weak point. The contextual action launches a crystal only during a valid weak-point window from a clearly marked reachable firing position. Boss targeting is automatic from that position; no additional aim gesture is required. A successful shot is an immediate authoritative hit with a cosmetic projectile, so projectile flight cannot spend a crystal then miss a closed window. Each accepted hit spends one crystal and removes one shield segment. Boss attacks and missed windows consume no crystal.
5. After the third hit, enter victory, grant the one-time completion reward, show the collection and offer another round or return to PrepX. No automatic replay or forced countdown.

Use the existing joystick and keyboard controls. Change the primary action label to “שיגור גביש” only when shooting is available. Touch release/cancel, blur, backgrounding, dialogs and pause clear held input. Maintain an input generation ID for every playable phase and resume. Discard queued actions from a previous generation, events received while frozen, and releases/clicks whose pointer/key began before the new phase was visibly ready. Accept an incoming answer/shot only from a fresh press after that generation is presented. No held pointer, delayed OS event, compatibility click or repeated key may answer or fire on the next screen.

## Proposed tuning defaults

These are implementation defaults, not independent user requirements: approximately 10 seconds for a successful bridge attempt, roughly 30 seconds for a clean boss fight, attack telegraph at least 800ms, vulnerability window at least 2 seconds, and at least 1200ms of collision protection after a hit. Active movement is the only timed part; reading is untimed. The first bridge and first boss cycle include a safe demonstration cue. Tune after mobile playtesting without changing progression/reward invariants.

Reduced motion uses stationary bridge geometry with a clear safe/unsafe signal and static attack-zone indicators instead of sweeping camera/shake effects. Every required target must remain reachable in this variant.

## Progress, resources and recovery

The state machine is explicit: introduction → staging-1 → bridge-1 → question-1 → feedback-1 → staging-2 → bridge-2 → question-2 → feedback-2 → staging-3 → bridge-3 → question-3 → feedback-3 → boss-ready → boss → victory. Staging/boss-ready have no hazards or running boost clocks and require an explicit Start/Continue action; they never auto-start while a collection/shop overlay is open. Collection/shop/pause are overlays on the current phase, not replacement progress states. Explicit Continue leaves feedback. Unclaimed gifts are a separate pending-reward set, not an implicit extra phase or a condition for reaching the boss; closing a gift view leaves its claim accessible from the collection.

- Boss crystals and spendable diamonds are distinct, named resources. One crystal is awarded for each question's first locked answer, even when wrong; crystals cannot be bought, exchanged, spent in the shop, lost to damage or awarded again by refresh/review.
- Store three crystal award IDs and three accepted boss-hit IDs. Available crystals = unique earned crystal IDs minus unique accepted hit IDs. A hit is valid only when it matches the current exposure window and the shield segment has not already been removed. The third accepted hit is one indivisible snapshot transaction: record the hit and crystal spend, remove the last shield segment, enter victory, claim/credit every still-pending stage gift, mark those claims, and credit/mark the one-time bonus diamond. No valid won-but-unsettled snapshot may exist.
- Each completed bridge-plus-question stage awards one collectible gift and one diamond, independent of correctness, with an idempotent stage reward ID. Claiming is explicit and must not award a second time. Keep pending rewards accessible at safe checkpoints and in the completed collection. Victory atomically settles any pending stage claims and adds one bonus diamond exactly once, so a missed claim cannot vanish or block the finale. Spending never removes displayed gifts or changes learning score/coins.
- In staging-1/2/3 and boss-ready only, the optional shop sells one-use shield and slow motion for one diamond each. At most one unused charge of each can be carried. A carried shield passively blocks the next physical damaging collision once; consumption, the prevented damage outcome and collision-grace state are saved in the same snapshot transaction. Checkpoint returns restore the latest committed resource state, not an older checkpoint inventory. An already-consumed shield cannot reappear after damage/reload; it never changes the verdict or heart consequence of a wrong academic answer. A separate labelled action activates a carried slow-motion charge only during a bridge or boss phase, consumes it once and sets five seconds remaining. Slow-motion duration uses unscaled active simulation time, while bridge hazards and all boss telegraph/attack/exposure clocks use that same time multiplied by 0.5. Rendering and collision use the same scaled hazard state; no independent wall-clock timeout may close the weak point early. Boost time does not advance during questions, feedback, pause, gift views or shops. Boosts never change grading or crystal costs. Cancelling or repeated activation cannot consume a second charge; the charge and remaining duration are one saved transaction. Purchases/consumption are saved atomically with balances. Remaining slow-motion time survives a checkpoint return and freezes during its safe recovery cue; it resumes on the next hazard-active frame, without a second charge deduction. The effect may span bridge stages and the boss; only remaining active duration matters. Opening the shop pauses hazards; active bridge, question, feedback and boss phases cannot purchase items. A damage checkpoint return is not a shop unlock; only the declared safe staging states allow purchases.
- A fall or damaging collision removes at most one heart per collision/grace interval and returns the player to the current bridge start or boss safe checkpoint. Keep answered questions, stage rewards, crystals, removed shield segments and purchases. At zero hearts, restore three hearts at that checkpoint; no restart or extra graded recovery questions.
- After three failed attempts on the same bridge or boss segment, enable a clearly explained assist variant. Damage and a missed exposure window both count as boss failures; the counter resets only after an accepted shield hit, not on refresh, a new attack cycle or zero-heart recovery. The assisted bridge stays open with hazards disabled on its traversable path. Assisted boss segments disable damage and keep their weak point open until deliberately hit; each removed segment creates a new eligible window ID. Assistance persists for the rest of that run, so repeated recovery cannot silently disable it. Offer assistance earlier on request. Assist never changes question wording, first-answer score or rewards. There is no maximum-attempt failure screen.
- A missed boss window advances to the next telegraphed cycle automatically. Each simulation step validates fresh actions against its current exposure (start inclusive, end exclusive), then resolves accepted hits, damage and expiry as one transaction. Victory from a third valid hit has priority over same-step damage/expiry. Otherwise a nonfinal valid hit is retained even if damage also occurs; any checkpoint return supersedes a timed cycle restart, issues one new exposure ID and returns to a safe telegraph. Old per-window flags and queued shots cannot be carried into that new cycle. A damage return cannot create another hit before the player has regained control; spawn points must be outside all attack zones.

Synthetic accounting example: three completed stages and boss victory earn four diamonds total. Buying a shield and slow motion spends two; the final balance is two, all three gifts remain displayed, and all three boss crystals have been spent on exactly three shield hits. The same progression remains possible with all three academic answers wrong and with no shop purchases.

## Persistence and compatibility

Use a separate versioned per-tab namespace, proposed `prepx-shadow-maze-arcade-v1`, so arcade work cannot overwrite `prepx-shadow-maze-session-v1`, normal-mode subject archives, Afterbell, formal exam drafts or practice best scores. Start/new-round and resume are explicit choices. A new run has a new run ID and fresh run-owned resources; show the previous run's summary and state clearly that the new wallet starts at zero. Keep the last completed summary, collected gifts and earned/spent totals as a read-only last-run record in the arcade namespace, separate from the new active run. Starting a new round must not remove the immediately preceding completed collection. No account-wide inventory or cross-run spending is implied.

Persist mode/version/run ID, pinned bank/content version, three selected question IDs/choice orders, phase/checkpoint, first answers, hearts, awarded stage/gift/crystal IDs, diamond purchases, item charges/remaining active time, removed boss segments, boss cycle/window ID, assist/failure state and completion payout marker. Derived amounts are reconstructed from validated records. Serialize each accepted progression/reward transaction into one versioned snapshot written under one storage key before presenting it as durable; do not split answer/crystal/phase or purchase/balance/charge writes across keys. Storage-write failure keeps the previous valid snapshot and triggers the unsaved warning. A missing whole uncommitted transaction may be retried from that snapshot; a partially inconsistent/corrupt transaction is not treated as legitimate completed play. Freeze active timers on pause/backgrounding; time away never moves hazards, expires a charge or consumes a boss window. On boss resume, place the player at the safe checkpoint, invalidate any previous exposure ID and issue a new saved window ID with a safe telegraph before permitting damage. Preserve accepted hit IDs and removed segments. If assistance is active, resume directly in its safe always-open variant.

Validate saved IDs, phase prerequisites and resource bounds. A phase ahead of its proven prerequisites is repaired back to the earliest unfinished stage, retaining valid earlier answers/rewards. Resume after a bridge fall repeats that bridge only if its stage has no locked answer; a locked answer and its atomic stage-complete record select the next unfinished stage, never an answered question. During the boss, require earned crystals = 3, accepted hits = removed shield segments, and available crystals = 3 minus removed segments. A completed three-hit record resolves to victory, never to a zero-crystal active boss. A saved boss with three proven stage records and zero, one or two validated hits resumes at the boss safe checkpoint with exactly three, two or one available crystals. It never searches for a nonexistent unfinished question stage. Do not infer a missing academic answer or crystal from a checkpoint, gift or purchase alone. If boss damage/hit data or resource records cannot be reconciled without inventing credits, preserve the raw record and offer a clear recovery/new-run choice; never silently overwrite it or enter an empty encounter. A storage failure shows the existing unsaved-progress warning and permits play without falsely promising refresh persistence. Never log student names or answers.
