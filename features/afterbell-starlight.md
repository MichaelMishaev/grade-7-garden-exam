# Afterbell · Starlight Relay

Students can already pick their subject, but every campus run asks them to repeat the same errands. The new primary entry starts an original constellation quest on the existing 3D campus. The classic blackout remains available. The design borrows free route choice, jump discoveries and collectible trails from platform adventures. Its setting, characters and art belong to PrepX.

| ID | Playable acceptance criterion | Check |
|---|---|---|
| AB1 | Pick an existing quiz and launch a relay. Choose any of three lit beacons first or change direction mid-run. | Browser |
| AB2 | A fresh relay selects one of three authored stories, excluding the previous layout on this device. Locations, mirror pattern and challenge assignments change. Resume restores the same story and collected sparks. Classic drafts still resume. | Unit and browser |
| AB3 | Walk to three separate sparks, jump at a halo or operate its accessible switch, and align three mirrors from a visible sequence. Misses retry without penalty or time pressure. Auto-walk reaches all targets. | Browser and unit |
| AB4 | Each marker opens its share of the selected complete quiz. First answers lock and show their explanations. A station lights even after an incorrect answer. The north gate opens only after three challenges and all answers. Game history uses the existing result path. | Unit and browser |
| AB5 | Gold rings, drifting sparks and a constellation at the gate respond to progress. Reduced motion keeps static geometry and the same playable cues. Hebrew RTL, mobile joystick, keyboard and guided walking remain available. | Browser and visual review |

Only the layout and challenge progress are new draft data. The existing attempt ID, quiz content, grading and privacy rules apply. Students can leave a question, return to its marker and continue. Human playtesting will determine whether they enjoy subsequent runs.
